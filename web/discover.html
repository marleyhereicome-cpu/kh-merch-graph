What is Kingdom Hearts?

Kingdom Hearts is a long-running action RPG series from Square Enix and Disney, weaving Disney's worlds and characters together with an original cast (and the occasional Final Fantasy face). It's been running since 2002 across more than a dozen games, and it's the reason this much merch exists in the first place. More on Wikipedia ↗

What's a shipping proxy?

Most Japanese shops and marketplaces (Mercari, Yahoo Auctions, the Square Enix store) only ship inside Japan. A shipping proxy is a paid service — Buyee and ZenMarket are two well-known ones — that buys the item on your behalf with a Japanese address, then forwards it to you. They add a service fee plus international shipping on top of the item's price. We're not affiliated with either — they're just the two most commonly used. This isn't something we do ourselves; we just point you to where an item shows up and let you take it from there.
