How this works

Each item links to its source (the maker's or an official shop's page, or reliable press). Prices are the maker's price at release, not today's resale price. Fan-made items are labelled separately from official merchandise so they're never confused. We take care to keep this accurate, but we can't guarantee it.

Merchgraph doesn't sell anything itself. Without a shelf of our own to fill, we can show you where an item really turns up and what it originally cost, wherever that is.
