Listing check

Found one on Mercari? Check it before you buy.

Paste the title of a Japanese secondhand listing (Mercari, Yahoo! Auctions, Suruga-ya, Mandarake…). You'll see which official item it most likely is, what the condition words mean, any signs of a bootleg, and how the price compares to the original.

We only read the text you paste. We never open the listing or store it.

How it works

  1. The title is matched against a hand-checked catalog of Kingdom Hearts merchandise, every entry linked to its source.
  2. Japanese condition words (未使用, 箱なし, タグ付き…) are explained, and wording that often signals a bootleg is flagged.
  3. If you add the price, it's compared with the item's original price. When the match isn't confident, we say so instead of guessing.
