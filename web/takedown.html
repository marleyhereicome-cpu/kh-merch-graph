Takedown requests

Merchgraph is a reference catalog of official and fan-made merchandise. We don’t sell anything. If you own the rights to something shown here, or you run a brand listed here, and you want an item, an image or your brand removed, please write to us. We will, as a rule, act promptly once we receive your request.

Send requests to
legal@merchgraph.app

What to include

  1. Your name, the organisation you act for, and how we can reach you (email address).
  2. What you own or represent: the work, trademark or brand concerned.
  3. The Merchgraph page(s) concerned (the URL of each item page, or the brand page), and whether you want the whole item, only the image, or the whole brand removed.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your request is accurate and that you are the rights holder or are authorised to act for them.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).

If something is missing we may write back to ask for it. Brands asking us to remove their own listings only need items 1, 3 and a short note that the request comes from the brand.

What happens next

  • We confirm that we received your request, and, as a rule, act on it promptly.
  • We remove the item, the image or the brand as requested, and reply when it is done.
  • We keep a record of each request (date received, who sent it, the pages concerned, what we did and when).

Repeated requests

If we receive valid requests about the same brand’s items more than once, we stop listing that brand.

DMCA designated agent

Our registration of a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office is in progress. Until it is complete, please send DMCA notices to legal@merchgraph.app.

Other questions

A mistake in an item (wrong price, wrong name)? Use the report form in the item’s details. For anything else, use the contact form.
